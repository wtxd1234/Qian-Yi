# 1. Chemistry (Code 5070 / 0620)

**A. Atomic Structure**
* **Proton Number:** Identity of the element (never changes).
* **Nucleon (Mass) Number:** Protons + Neutrons.
* **Isotopes:** Atoms of the same element with the same proton number but **different neutron numbers**.

**B. Bonding Types**
* **Ionic Bonding:** Happens between a **Metal** and a **Non-Metal**. Electrons are *transferred*.
    * *Example:* NaCl (Salt). High melting points, conducts electricity when molten/dissolved.
* **Covalent Bonding:** Happens between **Non-Metals** only. Electrons are *shared*.
    * *Example:* $CO_2$, $H_2O$. Low melting points, does not conduct electricity (usually).



**C. Acids, Bases & Salts**
* **Acid:** Proton ($H^+$) donor. pH < 7. Turns litmus **Red**.
* **Base/Alkali:** Proton ($H^+$) acceptor ($OH^-$ ions). pH > 7. Turns litmus **Blue**.
* **Neutralization:** $Acid + Base \rightarrow Salt + Water$.

# 2. Physics (Code 5054 / 0625)

**A. General Physics Formulas**
* **Speed:** $$v = \frac{d}{t}$$ (Distance / Time)
* **Density:** $$\rho = \frac{m}{V}$$ (Mass / Volume)
* **Force (Newton's 2nd Law):** $$F = ma$$ (Mass $\times$ Acceleration)
* **Weight:** $$W = mg$$ (Mass $\times$ Gravity). *Note: On Earth, g is approximately 10 or 9.8 m/s^2.*

**B. Electricity (Common struggle area)**
* **Current ($I$):** Measured in Amps (A). Flow of charge. $$I = \frac{Q}{t}$$
* **Voltage ($V$):** Measured in Volts (V). Energy per charge. $$V = \frac{E}{Q}$$
* **Resistance ($R$):** $$V = IR$$ (Ohm's Law).
* **Circuits:**
    * **Series:** Current is the *same* everywhere. Voltage splits.
    * **Parallel:** Voltage is the *same* across branches. Current splits.

# 3. Biology (Code 5090 / 0610)

**A. Cell Structure**
* **Nucleus:** Contains genetic material (DNA), controls cell activities.
* **Mitochondria:** Site of **aerobic respiration** (releases energy).
* **Ribosomes:** Site of protein synthesis.
* **Plant vs Animal:** Plants have a **Cell Wall**, **Chloroplasts**, and a **Large Vacuole**. Animals do not.

**B. Movement In and Out of Cells**
* **Diffusion:** Net movement of particles from **High Concentration $\rightarrow$ Low Concentration**. (Passive, no energy needed).
* **Osmosis:** Movement of **WATER** molecules from **High Water Potential $\rightarrow$ Low Water Potential** through a *partially permeable membrane*.
* **Active Transport:** Movement from **Low $\rightarrow$ High** concentration. Requires **Energy** (from respiration).

**C. Enzymes**
* Enzymes are **biological catalysts** made of protein.
* **Lock & Key Model:** The substrate shape must match the enzyme's active site perfectly.
* **Denaturation:** If temperature is too high or pH is extreme, the active site changes shape, and the enzyme stops working.
